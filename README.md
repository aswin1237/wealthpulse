## 📱 Features

- **📊 Financial Health Score (0 - 100)**: Real-time money assessment calculated from your savings rate and housing ratio.
- **💸 Daily & Monthly Expense Log**: Easily add, edit, search, and filter expenses with category tags and frequency indicators.
- **🏦 Annual Income & Tax Planner**: Tax deductions breakdown and 50/30/20 budget allocation guidance.
- **🤖 Wealthy AI Money Coach**: Interactive financial assistant that answers spending and savings queries using your live data.
- **🧮 4 Financial Calculators**:
  1. Compound Wealth Growth Estimator
  2. Split Bill & Tip Calculator
  3. Emergency Reserve Calculator
  4. Loan / Debt EMI Calculator
- **📲 Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar & FAB**: Mobile-app UI with single-thumb navigation.
- **⚡ Offline PWA Support**: Installable directly on iOS & Android home screens.
- **💾 LocalStorage Persistence & CSV Export**: Your data stays private in your browser.
